QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is The Chef's World?

We are a specialized promotional ecosystem that helps chefs, food creators, and food businesses grow their Instagram presence through coordinated organic social media exposure.

Are the audiences organic? Do you use bots?

Our audience is 100% organic. NO BOTS. NO FAKE ACCOUNTS. Our campaigns are designed around real exposure through authentic social audiences and food communities.

What does the follower/audience target mean?

The campaign target represents the minimum audience-growth commitment associated with your selected package. It is a benchmark for the scale of discovery we aim to provide through our network.

How does the creator ecosystem work?

Instead of relying on a single isolated post, we utilize our main platforms alongside a network of connected food creators and niche pages to provide repeated, coordinated visibility across multiple discovery points.

Can I send my own photographs and videos?

Yes, absolutely. We promote your authentic content. After selecting a package, you can email us your photos, Reels, and any specific creative instructions.

Which payment methods are accepted?

We accept PayPal, Venmo, Zelle, and Cash App. Payment instructions are provided securely via email after we review and confirm your campaign details.

Are engagement or sales guaranteed?

Audience and follower-growth commitments relate to the selected campaign target. Engagement (likes/comments), sales, bookings, and other business outcomes can vary significantly based on content quality, market conditions, and platform behavior, and are therefore not guaranteed.
